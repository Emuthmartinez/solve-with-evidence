Before the waitlist, filling a cancelled slot at Eastfield Community Clinic meant phone calls. Harbor's waitlist now offers a freed slot to the next patient.

"We used to lose most of a morning each week to phone tag over cancellations. The waitlist gave that back," says Dana Okafor, clinic manager at Eastfield Community Clinic.

Eastfield has 9 clinicians and has used Harbor since October 2025. Across Harbor, the waitlist has refilled 38% of cancelled slots since auto-offer shipped on 20 January 2026.
