Eastfield Community Clinic was drowning in cancellations until Harbor's waitlist changed everything.

"Harbor's waitlist saved us ten hours a week and paid for itself in the first month," says Dr. Dana Okafor, medical director at Eastfield Community Clinic.

Since switching in 2024, Eastfield has refilled 85% of cancelled slots and cut no-shows in half. Its 15 clinicians now spend that time with patients instead of on the phone.
