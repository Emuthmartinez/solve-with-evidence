Subject: Brief service interruption on 12 March

On 12 March we experienced a brief service interruption lasting about 20 minutes. A small number of reminders were delayed. We take this very seriously.

The root cause was a third-party SMS provider outage at Twilio, which has now been resolved. Fewer than 100 reminders were affected and no patients missed their appointments as a result.

We have since launched a public status page at status.tidewater.example and will email every clinic within 5 minutes of any future incident. Our engineering team, led by CTO Rahul Verma, has added 24/7 monitoring.

Thank you for your patience.

Mara Ellison
