We ended Q1 2026 with 212 paying clinics, up from 174 at the end of Q4 2025. We added 47 clinics and 9 cancelled; that count excludes 5 pilot clinics that were never billed. Monthly recurring revenue in March was $63,600, up from $50,460 in December 2025. Uptime was 99.82%, which includes the 12 March outage: 47 minutes of failed reminder sends. Across clinics using SMS reminders the no-show rate is 11.4%, against 17.9% for the same clinics before they turned reminders on. That figure is clinic-reported, not audited. Net revenue retention is not yet computed. Finance closes the books on 20 April. An earlier draft said 219 paying clinics; it included 7 clinics still in free trial, and 212 is the right figure.
