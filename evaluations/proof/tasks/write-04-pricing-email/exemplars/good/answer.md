Subject: Harbor prices from 1 July 2026

Harbor's prices change on 1 July 2026.

Today you pay $30 per clinician per month. The new prices are $34 per clinician per month for the first 10 clinicians at a clinic or group, and $26 per clinician per month for each clinician beyond 10.

You are a paying customer today, so nothing changes for you yet. Clinics that are paying customers on 30 June 2026 keep $30 per clinician until 31 December 2026, then move to the new prices.

An example: a clinic with 4 clinicians pays $120 a month today and will pay $136 a month at the new prices. For most clinics, those with 10 or fewer clinicians, the price goes up.

Why: SMS costs went up 22% in 2025, and larger groups asked for a volume price.

There is also a new annual billing option: pay for 12 months up front and get 2 months free.

Billing questions go to billing@tidewater.example.
