Subject: Exciting pricing updates!

We're excited to announce new, simpler pricing that means prices are going down for many of our customers!

From 1 June 2026, Harbor will cost just $26 per clinician per month for larger teams. Smaller clinics will pay $34. Existing customers are locked in at their current price for 12 months.

For example, a clinic with 20 clinicians will save over $100 a month. We're also adding a 25% discount for annual billing.

These changes let us invest in new features like our upcoming mobile app. Questions? Reach our support team any time, including weekends, at support@tidewater.example.
