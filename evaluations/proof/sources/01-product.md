# Harbor: product notes

Last edited 2 April 2026 by Mara Ellison.

- Harbor is appointment scheduling software for community clinics. Tidewater Labs makes it.
- The first paying clinic went live on 3 March 2025. General availability was 9 September 2025.
- Core features: a shared calendar across rooms and clinicians, SMS reminders, and a waitlist that offers a freed slot to the next patient.
- Waitlist auto-offer shipped on 20 January 2026. Before that, staff filled cancellations by phone.
- Harbor does not store clinical notes. It stores name, phone number, appointment time and visit type.
- Integrations: export to CSV. A two-way sync with the Medisoft record system is in private beta with 6 clinics. No other record-system integration exists.
- Team: 7 people. 4 engineers, 1 designer, 1 support lead, and Mara Ellison (founder and CEO).
- Support hours: weekdays 8:00 to 18:00 Central Time. There is no weekend support.
