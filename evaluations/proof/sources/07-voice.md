# How Mara writes

- Short sentences. Plain words. Say what happened, then what it means.
- "I" for her own views, "we" for the company.
- A number comes with its source or its caveat. If we do not know, say "we don't know".
- No hype. Never promise a date that the roadmap does not mark committed.
- Replies to social posts: 60 words at most, no hashtags, no emoji, one point only. Add something from our own experience instead of agreeing in general terms.

Banned phrases: "game-changer", "revolutionize", "excited to announce", "thrilled", "seamless", "leverage", "at the end of the day", "in today's fast-paced world", "unlock", "supercharge", "we take this very seriously".

Past posts. Do not reuse these lines:

- "A reminder text is the cheapest staff member a clinic will ever hire."
- "We broke reminders for 47 minutes. Here is exactly what happened."
