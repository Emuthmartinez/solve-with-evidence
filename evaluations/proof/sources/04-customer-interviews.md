# Customer interviews, March 2026

Notes by Ines Halloran (support lead). Quotes are verbatim from recorded calls. Each customer agreed to be quoted by name.

## Dana Okafor, clinic manager, Eastfield Community Clinic (9 clinicians), 18 March

- Using Harbor since October 2025.
- "We used to lose most of a morning each week to phone tag over cancellations. The waitlist gave that back."
- Wants Spanish-language SMS reminders. "About a third of our patients would rather get the text in Spanish."
- Was affected by the 12 March outage. "Nobody told us until we called. Tell us first next time."

## Luis Ferreira, operations director, Northgate Family Health (3 sites, 31 clinicians), 21 March

- Using Harbor since January 2026. Moved from paper diaries at two sites.
- "The shared calendar is the reason we bought it. I can see all three sites on one screen."
- Wants the Medisoft sync and is in the private beta. "Double entry is the one thing my front desk still complains about."
- On price: the per-clinician price is fair for small sites but "gets steep for us at 31 clinicians".

## Priya Raman, front-desk lead, Mill Road Clinic (4 clinicians), 25 March

- Using Harbor since February 2026.
- "Reminders cut our no-shows, but I can't tell you by how much. We never counted before."
- Wants patients to be able to reschedule from the reminder text. Today the patient has to call.
- Did not notice the 12 March outage.
