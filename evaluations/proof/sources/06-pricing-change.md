# Pricing change, effective 1 July 2026

Decided 2 April 2026. Not yet announced to customers.

Current price: $30 per clinician per month, billed monthly. No minimum.

New prices from 1 July 2026:

- $34 per clinician per month for the first 10 clinicians at a clinic or group.
- $26 per clinician per month for each clinician beyond 10.
- Annual billing option: pay for 12 months up front and get 2 months free.

Grandfathering: clinics that are paying customers on 30 June 2026 keep $30 per clinician until 31 December 2026, then move to the new prices.

Why: SMS costs went up 22% in 2025, and larger groups asked for a volume price.

Worked examples at the new monthly prices:

- 4 clinicians: 4 × $34 = $136 a month (was $120).
- 31 clinicians: 10 × $34 + 21 × $26 = $886 a month (was $930).

Do not say prices are "going down". For most clinics, those with 10 or fewer clinicians, the price goes up.

Billing questions go to billing@tidewater.example.
