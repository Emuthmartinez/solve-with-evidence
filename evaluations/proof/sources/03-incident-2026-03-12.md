# Incident notes: reminder outage, 12 March 2026

Written by Kofi Mensah (engineering) on 13 March 2026. Reviewed by Mara Ellison.

Timeline, Central Time:

- 07:42 A database migration for the waitlist feature started.
- 07:48 The reminder worker began failing. The migration held a lock on the appointments table.
- 08:05 The first clinic reported that morning reminders had not gone out.
- 08:31 The migration was cancelled and the worker restarted.
- 08:35 Reminders were flowing again. Total: 47 minutes of failed reminder sends.

Impact:

- 1,930 SMS reminders were not sent on time. 1,610 were sent late, between 08:35 and 09:10. 320 were for appointments that had already started and were dropped.
- 58 clinics had at least one dropped reminder.
- Scheduling itself stayed up. No appointment data was lost or changed.

Cause: the migration ran during the morning reminder window. Our runbook said to run migrations after 20:00, but nothing enforced it.

Fixes:

- Done 13 March: the deploy tool blocks migrations between 06:00 and 20:00.
- Done 17 March: the reminder worker retries for 30 minutes instead of failing after one attempt.
- Not done: a status page. Planned, no date.

We do not know how many patients missed appointments because of the dropped reminders. Clinics do not report that to us.
